# Asset and software credits

NIVET ROYALE uses the supplied Tilted Towers, Retail Row and Lazy Links Blender scenes, character, weapon and bus models, grass, cliff, road and sidewalk textures, and trees and plants from the supplied nature pack. The forward run uses the supplied Running.fbx. Locomotion, airborne and pistol motion include retargeted clips from [Quaternius's Universal Animation Library](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html), released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The glider, supplemental weapons, remaining pose layers and synthesized sounds are original approximations.

This work is based on [Plumgerman](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/plumgerman-cb4822ae8d4946bd9b8375ac70f33385) by [Gh4xt3r](https://sketchfab.com/Gh4xt3r), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The derivative normalizes scale/orientation, caps textures and applies procedural gameplay poses. Other supplied model packages did not include licensing notices; original authorship was not established by the asset intake.
