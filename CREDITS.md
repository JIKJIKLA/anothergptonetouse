# Asset and software credits

NIVET ROYALE uses the supplied Tilted Towers, Retail Row and Lazy Links Blender scenes, character, weapon and bus models, grass, cliff, road and sidewalk textures, and trees and plants from the supplied nature pack. The treasure chest uses the original geometry and color and normal textures from the supplied Tilted Towers scene, with a separate lid for opening. The forward run uses the supplied Running.fbx. Jogging, sprinting, crouch movement, jump transitions, pistol poses and the chest interaction include retargeted clips from [Quaternius's Universal Animation Library](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html), released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The game uses effects selected from the supplied Fortnite v22.00 Battle Royale and Save the World audio and the supplied Lobby Classic track. The glider, supplemental items, remaining pose layers and synthesized fallback sounds are original approximations.

This work is based on [Plumgerman](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/plumgerman-cb4822ae8d4946bd9b8375ac70f33385) by [Gh4xt3r](https://sketchfab.com/Gh4xt3r), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The derivative normalizes scale/orientation, caps textures and applies procedural gameplay poses. Other supplied model packages did not include licensing notices; original authorship was not established by the asset intake.
